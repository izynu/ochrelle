Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026. Applies to the Ochrelle website and showcase.

Scope

This policy covers the Ochrelle website and its showcase pages. It does not cover third-party sites linked from here, such as external documentation or the agentskills.io specification. Those sites have their own policies.

What we collect

This site is static. Pages are plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript served from a web server. The site itself does not collect, store, or process personal data. Your hosting provider may record standard server logs, such as IP address, requested URL, user agent, and timestamp, for security and reliability. Log retention is governed by the hosting provider's own policy.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies and uses no cookie-based sessions. No consent banner is needed because no tracking technologies are present.

Analytics and advertising

The site embeds no analytics scripts, no advertising networks, and no third-party trackers. If analytics are ever added, this page will be updated before that change ships.

Fonts and assets

Fonts (IBM Plex Sans and JetBrains Mono) are self-hosted as part of the site files. They are served from the same origin as the site, so no request is sent to a font vendor when you visit.

Third parties

The only third-party interaction is your connection to the hosting provider that serves the pages. The site makes no background requests to other domains.

Children

This site is technical documentation for software developers. It is not directed at children, and no personal information is collected from any visitor.

Your rights

Because the site collects no personal data, there is nothing to export, correct, or delete. Requests about server-side hosting logs should be directed to the hosting provider.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be published on this page with an updated effective date. The effective date shown above reflects the current version.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be opened as an issue in the Ochrelle repository (github.com/izynu/ochrelle), or sent through the contact channel listed in the repository README.
