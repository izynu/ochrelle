Skeuomorphism

Visual style

Digital objects imitate physical ones: brushed metal, soft depth, controls that look pressable because they look holdable. Affordance is carried by material rendering rather than by flat convention.

Choose when the interface gains from tactile familiarity - media, tools, games - and users should read function straight from form.

Neumorphism

Visual style

One monochrome surface, extruded and inset by twin soft shadows. Components appear pressed into the same material as the page itself; hierarchy comes from light direction, not from color.

Choose when you need a calm, physical control surface - settings, remotes, kiosks - and color must stay out of the way.

Glassmorphism

Visual style

Frosted layers float above vivid content - blurred, saturated, edge-lit. Depth is literal: you can see the world behind the glass, softly. The blur is progressively enhanced; without it, the card falls back to a solid frosted fill.

Choose when a floating layer must stay connected to the content behind it - notifications, overlays, media HUDs.

Claymorphism

Visual style

Chunky, toy-like volumes in pastel fills, with a double shadow - a soft drop plus an inner light along the bottom edge. Everything looks squeezable, and interaction answers with a spring.

Choose when warmth beats efficiency - habit trackers, kids’ products, friendly onboarding.

Neo-brutalism

Visual style

Raw black borders, hard offset shadows, flat saturated blocks, and type that shouts. Honesty over polish - the structure itself is the decoration, and every press is mechanical.

Choose when personality and zero ambiguity matter - event pages, independent publications, tools that reject corporate gloss.

Minimalism

Visual style

Every element earns its place: hairline rules, a single accent, a precise type scale, and whitespace doing the grouping. Feedback stays quiet - 200ms fades, nothing more.

Choose when content must outrank chrome - reading, writing, and reference tools where trust is built through restraint.

Maximalism

Visual style

More, but composed: layered type, patterned fields, stickers, and saturated hues arranged around one undeniable focal point. A readability island keeps the copy legible inside the noise.

Choose when the job is to be unforgettable - drops, campaigns, merch, and anything fighting for attention in a crowded feed.

Liquid Glass (web approximation)

Platform material

Translucent controls sit on a living backdrop: blur, saturation boost, and a specular top edge make the interface feel like one continuous optical layer. This is a web approximation - the native material’s true lensing and refraction are not reproduced.

Choose when your product lives on Apple platforms - or borrows their material language - and the backdrop is part of the content.

Bento Grid

Layout pattern

A 12-column grid of rounded tiles with varied spans. Size encodes priority - the biggest tile carries the most important fact - and DOM order mirrors visual order. The grid reflows from 12 columns to 6 to 2 as the viewport narrows.

Choose when many small facts must scan as one glanceable composition - dashboards, profile pages, status screens.

Spatial UI (2D spatial-inspired)

Interaction paradigm

Elements occupy depth: planes at different translateZ respond to pointer position with restrained parallax - never more than 8px - and hover lifts a card out of the scene. Motion expresses hierarchy, not decoration.

Choose when hierarchy can be physical space - showcases, concept pieces, visionOS-inspired screens.
